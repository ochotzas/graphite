Selectors

Dropdowns and pickers for choosing one thing, several things or a time. Every panel opens from its control on the spring and works fully from the keyboard. Specs in components.md › Inputs.

Searchable select

Type a name or a role.

For long lists (more than ~8 items). Matches are bold; ↑↓ move, Enter picks, Escape restores the last choice.

Multi-select

Shown on the dish and the printed menu.

The list stays open while picking. Backspace in an empty field removes the last tag.
